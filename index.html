About me

Hello, I’m Maria.

I help older adults and people who need a little extra support live safely, comfortably and with dignity at home — and I help their families rest easier knowing someone caring is there.

Every person I care for has their own story, routines and preferences. I take the time to listen, get to know your loved one, and give care the way they like it — with patience, honesty and genuine kindness. Families can always reach me, and I keep you updated on how things are going.

How I can help

Care that fits your loved one’s day

Support can be as light or as hands-on as your family needs. Here’s what I can help with:

  • 💬

    Companionship

    Friendly conversation, games, walks, reading, hobbies and simply being there — so no one feels alone.

  • 🛁

    Personal care help

    Gentle, respectful help with bathing, dressing, grooming and other daily routines.

  • 🍲

    Meal preparation

    Healthy, tasty meals prepared to your loved one’s tastes and dietary needs, plus help at mealtimes.

  • ⏰

    Medication reminders

    Friendly reminders to take medications on schedule, following the plan from their doctor and family.

  • 🦯

    Mobility help

    Steady support getting up, moving around the home, and staying safe to help prevent falls.

  • 🌿

    Respite for families

    Take a break, go to work, or rest — knowing your loved one is in caring hands while you’re away.

  • 🧺

    Light housekeeping

    Tidying up, laundry, and keeping the home clean, safe and comfortable.

  • 🚗

    Errands & appointments
